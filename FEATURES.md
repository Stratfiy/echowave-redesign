# Decibyl.ai — Feature by Feature

*(Rename in progress: renamed from "EchoWave" to "Decibyl.ai" in product/marketing docs. The underlying codebase still says EchoWave — that engineering pass is scoped but on hold. See ROADMAP.md.)*

## Voice Agent Builder

Design your phone rep's entire conversation — greeting, qualifying questions, objection handling, escalation, close — as a visual flow, not a wall of prompt text. Describe the use case in a sentence and the agent scaffolds itself, ready to edit. A new hire needs weeks of training and a script binder before they're client-ready; a new Decibyl.ai agent is live and consistent from its first call.

## Always-On, Infinitely Scalable

No shifts, no sick days, no attrition, no 2am staffing gap. One call or ten thousand simultaneous calls run on the same cost curve — a human team needs weeks of hiring and ramp-up to handle a volume spike; Decibyl.ai just takes the next call.

## 100% Quality Coverage, Every Call

A human QA team samples maybe 1–2% of calls. Every Decibyl.ai call is automatically scored against your criteria — no exceptions, no bad-day variance, no rep quietly drifting off-script. You get full visibility into 100% of your conversations, not a sample.

## Live Actions Mid-Call (Tools & Integrations)

The agent doesn't put callers on hold to "check the system" — it looks up the account, books the slot, updates the CRM, or calls your backend live, in the conversation, via HTTP tools or MCP. A human rep tabs between five systems; the agent is already wired into all of them.

## Instant Multilingual

Adding a language is a voice and language selection, not a hiring campaign. Serve callers in their language without recruiting, training, or scheduling bilingual staff.

## Bulk Outbound at a Scale No Team Can Match

Upload a contact list and the agent dials thousands of leads in parallel, personalized per contact from your own data — respecting concurrency and retry rules automatically. Matching that volume with human dialers means a call floor, shift schedules, and a management layer Decibyl.ai replaces entirely.

## Takes Real Action Mid-Call, Not Just Conversation

Beyond talking, the agent can send DTMF keypad tones to navigate a menu, send an SMS, create a calendar event, or send an email — live, during the call, without a human touching a keyboard. Connect a Google account once and the agent can book straight into it: sign in, and every available action shows up ready to use, the same way you'd add an app integration anywhere else.

## Knows Your Entire Playbook, Instantly

Upload your product docs, policies, or pricing sheets and the agent references them accurately on every call from day one. No onboarding period, no forgetting what it learned last month, no inconsistency between reps who trained at different times.

## Every Call, Fully Transparent

Full transcripts, recordings, and step-by-step tracing of exactly what the agent heard and why it responded the way it did — for every single call. A human agent's account of a difficult call is their memory of it; Decibyl.ai's is a complete, auditable record.

## Built by Describing It, Not Coding It

Tell it what you want in plain English, or point an AI coding assistant like Claude Code directly at your workspace via MCP to build and edit agents. Standing up a new use case is a conversation, not a dev sprint.

## You Pay Providers Directly — No Hidden Markup

Bring your own OpenAI, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, or other provider keys. Decibyl.ai charges a flat $0.02/minute platform fee on top, prepaid — nothing baked into a blended per-minute rate you can't see into. You know exactly what you're paying for and to whom, and new accounts start with a $10 credit to try it before paying anything.

## Hands Off to a Human When It Actually Matters

The agent isn't a replacement wall — it can transfer live to a person for the calls that genuinely need one, seamlessly, mid-conversation. You get the coverage and cost of automation with a safety net for the moments that need a human touch.

## One Agent, Every Channel

The same agent that answers your phone line can be embedded directly on your website as a voice widget. A human rep is in one place at a time; your agent is everywhere your customers already are.

## Works With the Phone System You Already Have

Connects to Twilio, Vonage, Telnyx, Plivo, and other major providers, or your own SIP trunk — no forced migration off infrastructure you've already invested in.
