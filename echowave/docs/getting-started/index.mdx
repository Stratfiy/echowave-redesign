## About Decibyl

**Decibyl is a voice AI platform for building and running production phone agents.** You design a conversation as a flow, connect a phone number or drop a talk button on your website, and Decibyl runs the call — speech recognition, the model, the voice, the tools it calls mid-conversation, and the recording and transcript afterwards.

It is built for teams selling and supporting in India, which shows up where it matters:

- **Indian languages, properly** — Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati and more, including callers who switch language mid-sentence.
- **Your model, your keys** — bring your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Sarvam, Deepgram or ElevenLabs keys, or use ours. Mix them: your LLM with our speech, or the reverse.
- **Telephony that works here** — Plivo, Twilio, Telnyx, Vonage and Cloudonix, or a managed number we buy and configure for you.
- **A flow you can read** — a graph of nodes rather than one long prompt, so you can see where a conversation went and change that one step.
- **Built for assistants** — an MCP server, so Claude Code or Codex can build and edit agents directly.

## Pricing

**$0.02 per minute** of connected call time, billed per second.

That is the platform fee. On top of it you pay for what the call consumes:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Platform** | $0.02/min of connected time, billed per second |
| **Model, speech-to-text, text-to-speech** | Your own provider keys, billed by them — or ours, billed through us |
| **Telephony** | Your carrier account, billed by them — or a managed number from us |

No seat licence, no minimum commitment, and nothing to pay for agents you build but never call. Billing is prepaid: top up, and usage draws down. See [Billing](/account/billing) for invoices, GST and payment methods.

## Running Decibyl in your own environment

For regulated teams and high-scale deployments, Decibyl can run inside your own cloud — your VPC, your data, your retention policy, with call audio never leaving your perimeter. That is a commercial arrangement rather than a download; [talk to us](https://decibyl.ai) about it.

The [Deployment](/deployment/introduction) section covers how the platform is put together, which is what you want if you are the person operating it.
