## About Decibyl

**Decibyl is where your team works alongside AI co-workers.** You hire a bot for a job — answering the phone, replying on chat and WhatsApp, answering staff and customers from your own documents, filing a task every morning, watching for an order and acting on it — and it does that job on whatever channel the job needs, with a person able to see, confirm and correct every step. A manager bot, Decibyl, builds, edits and tests your bots from one conversation, and the bots hand work to each other on a shared board.

**Voice is one channel, and it was the first.** It is still the hardest, which is why so much of Decibyl is telephony and low-latency speech. But a bot is not a phone line: the same bot answers a WhatsApp message, reads a PDF you drop into the chat, runs on a schedule, fires when a webhook arrives, and asks a colleague bot for help — all in one place your team can watch.

It is built for teams selling and supporting in India, which shows up where it matters:

- **Indian languages, properly** — Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati and more, including people who switch language mid-sentence.
- **Your model, your keys** — bring your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Sarvam, Deepgram or ElevenLabs keys, or use ours. Mix them: your LLM with our speech, or the reverse.
- **Every channel a job needs** — the phone (Plivo, Twilio, Telnyx, Vonage, Cloudonix, or a managed number we configure for you), WhatsApp, web chat, email and webhooks.
- **People in the loop** — a thread per bot, a board the team and the bots share, and cards a person confirms before anything reaches a customer, with an undo window.
- **Built for assistants** — an MCP server, so Claude Code or Codex can build and edit bots directly.

## Running Decibyl in your own environment

For regulated teams and high-scale deployments, Decibyl can run inside your own cloud — your VPC, your database, your retention policy, your object storage. Speech recognition, the language model and speech synthesis are still calls to the providers you configure, so audio and transcripts reach them wherever the application runs; which providers, and in which region, is yours to choose. That is a commercial arrangement rather than a download; [talk to us](https://decibyl.ai) about it, and we will walk you through the architecture and what operating it involves.
