Decibyl is a hosted platform. There is nothing to install and nothing to run — you need an account, a browser, and a few decisions made before your first call connects.

## An account and a balance

Sign up at [app.decibyl.ai](https://app.decibyl.ai). Every account is prepaid: calls draw down a rupee balance, and **calls stop when it reaches zero**. Top up from **Billing** before you start testing.

Keep an eye on **Credit lasts** on the [Spend tab](/account/analytics) once you have traffic — it projects your remaining days at the current burn rate and turns red at seven.

<Note>
Add your billing profile (legal name, address, and GSTIN if you have one) before your first top-up. The GSTIN determines whether you are charged CGST+SGST or IGST, and it is stamped onto the invoice at the moment of payment — it cannot be added to a document already issued. See [Billing](/account/billing).
</Note>

## Which models your agent runs on

Two options, and you can mix them per slot — transcription on one arrangement, the language model on another.

| | Managed | Bring your own key |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Nothing to do | Add your provider key |
| Model billing | On your Decibyl balance | Direct from the provider |
| Platform fee | $0.02/min | $0.02/min |
| Choice of model | What we offer | Anything your account can reach |

Managed is the faster start. BYOK makes sense once volume is high enough that provider pricing matters to you, or when you need a specific model. Set either up in **Model Configurations** — see [Inference providers](/configurations/inference-providers).

<Note>
Agents running on your own keys are billed by that provider directly, so their model spend does **not** appear on the Tokens tab in Decibyl. An empty **By model** table on an account making calls usually just means BYOK.
</Note>

### If you are calling Indian numbers

Two things gate outbound dialling in India, and both take real time to clear — start them early rather than on the day you want to launch:

- **KYC.** Submit your business documents under **Verification**. Carriers will not issue or activate a number without it. The registered address on your incorporation certificate has to match what you enter, exactly.
- **DND and calling hours.** Do not dial anyone who has not asked to hear from you, and stay inside 9am–9pm. This is TRAI/TCCCPR territory and the exposure is regulatory, not commercial.
