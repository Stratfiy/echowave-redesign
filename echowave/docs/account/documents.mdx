Decibyl is provided without charge for now, so there is nothing to buy and no checkout. This page is the record of money that **was** paid before that: the documents issued for it stay under **Documents** for as long as the account exists, and nothing is deleted.

## Receipts, invoices and credit notes

Every payment produced a **receipt voucher**: a GST document with your registered name, GSTIN, place of supply, SAC 998314 and the tax split, as CGST 9% + SGST 9% when your registered state is Tamil Nadu and IGST 18% otherwise. Our Udyam (MSME) registration number prints on every invoice.

**Refunds and corrections** are issued as a **credit note** against the original document, in its own numbered series, reversing the tax in the same proportion.

**Outside India.** Documents for an account outside India are a zero-rated export of services under our Letter of Undertaking: the document states "Supply meant for export under LUT without payment of IGST", carries no GST line, and shows your country and address.

Under **Documents**, each one can be downloaded as a PDF or sent to your email for documents. The payment history sits beside them, with the reference your bank shows.

## Complete the details on your documents

Under **Documents → Details on your documents**, fill in your registered business name, GSTIN, address and email for documents. An organization Admin or Owner can change them.

This matters more than it looks:

- **Your GSTIN and state code decide the tax split.** A customer in the same state as us pays CGST + SGST; anywhere else in India pays IGST. The total is the same either way, but the split is what your accountant files.
- **A document issued before you complete this has no customer details on it**, which makes it useless for claiming input credit. Documents already issued keep the details they were issued with.
- **The email for documents is where they are sent.** Without one they are still issued and downloadable, but nothing is emailed.
- **Enterprise accounts** can add a purchase-order number and agreed payment terms, and they print on every document issued afterwards.

If documents were issued before you filled this in, open **Documents** and use the send button on each one. They will go to your email for documents with the PDF attached.
