Decibyl is prepaid. You add credit, calls draw it down, and you are never invoiced for more than you have already paid for.

## What you pay

**$0.02 per minute** of connected call time, billed in **15-second pulses**. Time is counted from answer to hangup — ringing, busy signals and unanswered calls cost nothing.

A 62-second call bills 75 seconds, not two whole minutes. Most platforms round every call up to the next whole minute; on short, high-volume traffic that rounding alone is worth about 17% of a 45-second call.

That is the platform fee. Alongside it you pay for what the call consumes:

| Component | Billed by |
| --- | --- |
| **Platform** | Decibyl, at $0.02/min |
| **LLM, speech-to-text, text-to-speech** | Your provider, if you use your own keys — otherwise Decibyl |
| **Telephony minutes** | Your carrier, if you bring your own account — otherwise Decibyl |
| **Managed phone number** | Decibyl, monthly per number |

There is no seat licence and no minimum commitment. Agents you build and never call cost nothing.

### Your keys or ours

Every model slot — LLM, transcription, voice — is independently either **yours** or **ours**.

- **Your key (BYOK).** You pay the provider directly at whatever rate you have with them. Decibyl adds nothing for that component.
- **Our key (managed).** Usage appears on your Decibyl invoice, and you do not need an account with the provider at all.

Mixing is normal and supported: your own OpenAI key for the LLM, our Sarvam for Indian-language speech, for example. See [Inference providers](/configurations/inference-providers).

## The Starter plan

**₹2,999 a month** — one phone number and ₹2,500 of call balance, collected by your bank on a standing instruction.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Call balance each month | ₹2,500 |
| Phone numbers included | 1 |
| Extra numbers | ₹499 a month each |

GST is added to the plan price at collection, so a domestic account is debited ₹3,538.82.

The balance arrives when each monthly collection is confirmed by your bank, not when you authorise the mandate. The number's rent is already inside the price — it is not taken from the balance as well.

**The included number is an entitlement, not a limit on how many you can have.** Claim your first number at no extra cost; a second number opens its own ₹499 monthly rental, drawn from your credit balance.

Start it from **Billing → Plan**. One plan at a time — to move to a different one, cancel the current mandate first.

## Adding credit

**Billing → Add credit.** Choose an amount and pay by card, UPI, net banking or wallet through Razorpay.

Credit is bought in steps of **₹100** — ₹100, ₹200, ₹500 and so on. The minimum is ₹100.

Credit appears once the payment is confirmed by Razorpay's servers, not when the payment window closes. That is usually a second or two. The balance on screen re-reads itself rather than assuming — a browser saying "paid" is not proof of payment.

<Note>
GST is added at checkout on top of the credit you buy. Buying ₹500 of credit charges ₹590 and gives you ₹500 of usage; the ₹90 is tax, not a fee.
</Note>

## Invoices and receipts

Every payment produces a **receipt voucher** — a GST document with your registered name, GSTIN, place of supply and the tax split.

They appear under **Billing → Documents**, where each one can be downloaded as a PDF or sent to your billing email.

### Complete your billing profile first

Under **Billing → Billing details**, fill in your registered business name, GSTIN, address and billing email.

This matters more than it looks:

- **Your GSTIN and state code decide the tax split.** A customer in the same state as us pays CGST + SGST; anywhere else in India pays IGST. The total is the same either way, but the split is what your accountant files.
- **A document issued before you complete this has no customer details on it**, which makes it useless for claiming input credit.
- **Your billing email is where documents are sent.** Without one they are still issued and downloadable, but nothing is emailed.

If documents were issued before you filled this in, open **Billing → Documents** and use the send button on each one — they will go to your billing email with the PDF attached.

## Running out of credit

When your balance runs low you get an email warning before anything stops, sent once a day at 09:00 IST to everyone on the account.

**Calling pauses once your balance falls below ₹20**, and resumes the moment you top up. Nothing is deleted — your agents, numbers, recordings and transcripts stay exactly as they are.

The floor is ₹20 rather than zero on purpose. A call's cost is not known until it ends, so an account allowed to start a call on its last rupee would finish that call overdrawn. Stopping a little above zero means the last call you place is always one you had the credit for.

For a **managed phone number**, the monthly rental is collected from your balance. If it cannot be collected the number is suspended after seven days and becomes eligible for release after 45 — which is why the warning email exists, and why autopay is worth setting up.

## Autopay

**Billing → Autopay** sets up a UPI mandate so the monthly number rental collects automatically, without you needing to remember a top-up.

The mandate is authorised once by you and covers the rental only. It does not top up your usage credit — that stays a deliberate act.

## Seeing where the money went

**Agent Runs** lists every call with its duration and cost. **Analytics** aggregates that by day, agent and campaign.

If a number surprises you, start with the run: it shows the per-call breakdown of platform time, model usage and telephony.
