Decibyl is prepaid and self-serve. A plan gives you credits every month, your bots draw them down, top-ups never expire, and you are never invoiced for more than you have already paid for.

## Plans

Decibyl is self-serve: pick a plan on **Billing → Plan**, pay by card, UPI or net banking, and start. Every plan is a monthly bundle of **credits** — one credit is ₹0.50 — that every kind of work draws on: a call, a text reply, a knowledge answer, a routine run, a tool call. There is no per-minute platform fee and no seat licence.

**Everyday is the personal plan.** It is for one person and their bots on chat, WhatsApp, email and routines — no phone line. It is the only paid plan that can be bought without a GSTIN, and outside India it is sold for **$10 a month**.

**Business is the first plan that answers the phone.** From Business up, a plan includes phone numbers as an entitlement: claim the included ones at no extra cost, and any number past that opens its own monthly rental, drawn from your credit balance. A plan is invoiced as a business supply, so it needs your GSTIN.

Plan credits arrive when each collection is confirmed by your bank, and unused plan credits expire at the end of the cycle; [top-up credits](#adding-credit) never do. One plan at a time — to move rungs, change the plan from **Billing → Plan** and the next collection is at the new price.

<Note>
**Accounts that started before the ladder** are on the Starter plan (₹2,999 a month, one number, ₹2,500 of balance). It is Business at the same price and carries Business's caps; nothing changes until you choose to move.
</Note>

### What a call costs

Voice is metered per minute of connected time, from answer to hangup — ringing, busy signals and unanswered calls cost nothing — and the minute is a fixed number of credits that already includes transcription, the language model, the voice and, on a managed number, the carrier:

| Plan | Credits a minute | In rupees |
|---|---:|---:|
| Business | 13 | ₹6.50 |
| Growth | 12 | ₹6.00 |
| Scale | 11 | ₹5.50 |

Time is counted in **15-second pulses**: a 62-second call bills 75 seconds, not two whole minutes. Most platforms round every call up to the next whole minute; on short, high-volume traffic that alone is worth about 17% of a 45-second call.

### Your keys or ours

Every model slot — LLM, transcription, voice — is independently either **yours** or **ours**.

- **Our key (managed).** The slot is inside the per-minute credit price, and you do not need an account with the provider at all.
- **Your key (BYOK).** You pay the provider directly at whatever rate you have with them. Decibyl charges nothing for that slot: a call is then metered on the components we still run, not at the bundle's flat price.

Mixing is normal and supported: your own OpenAI key for the LLM, our Sarvam for Indian-language speech, for example. See [Inference providers](/configurations/inference-providers).

## Free credits

Every new account can earn **1,000 free credits**, one step at a time. Each step pays as you do it, and the Home screen keeps the list until every step is done.

| Step | Credits |
|---|---:|
| Verify your email | 150 |
| Build your first bot | 150 |
| Put it on a channel: send or answer a first message | 150 |
| Have a real conversation: a call or a message exchange with a customer | 200 |
| Schedule a routine, and let it run once | 200 |
| Move in: upload a document, or invite a teammate | 150 |

Free credits are a gift, not a purchase: no invoice, no GST. They are spent like any other credit. The first routine run is free of its usual 2-credit charge, so the reward and the bill never arrive together.

### Invite a business

Every account has a referral link, on Home and under Billing. When a business signs up through it and makes its **first payment**, you both receive **200 credits**. Nothing is paid on a signup alone. A referral is attributed once, at signup, and cannot be added later.

You can earn on up to 20 paid referrals a month; the business you invited always receives theirs. Partner accounts earn commission on their referrals instead, never both. Internal Decibyl accounts earn nothing.

## Adding credit

**Billing → Add credit.** Pick a pack, or enter an amount, and pay by card, UPI, net banking or wallet through Razorpay.

| Pack | Credits | Extra |
|---|---:|---|
| ₹999 | 2,000 | — |
| ₹4,999 | 10,500 | 500 credits (5%) |
| ₹19,999 | 44,000 | 4,000 credits (10%) |

Accounts on the Campus plan, and early-adopter accounts our team has marked, also see a **₹500 pack for 1,000 credits**.

**Outside India, packs are in US dollars.** An account whose billing profile puts it outside India sees the dollar ladder instead, pays by international card through the same Razorpay checkout, and receives a zero-rated export receipt voucher stating the rupee value at the day's rate:

| Pack | Credits | Extra |
|---|---:|---|
| $12 | 2,000 | — |
| $60 | 10,500 | 500 credits (5%) |
| $240 | 44,000 | 4,000 credits (10%) |

Dollar accounts buy packs only; there is no free dollar amount. Until dollar payments are switched on, the Billing page says so and support adds credit directly.

**Top-up credits never expire.** They sit in their own pool and are spent only after your plan's credits for the cycle are used up. A free amount can also be entered in steps of **₹100** (minimum ₹1,000) and buys credits at face: ₹0.50 a credit.

Each plan caps how many top-up credits an account can hold at once; the Billing page names the next plan up when you reach it.

### Promo codes

A promo code goes in the **Promo code** box before you pay. A percent or amount code takes that off the price, GST is charged on the discounted amount, and the receipt voucher shows the discount as its own line. A bonus-credit code adds credits when the payment lands. On a plan, only bonus-credit codes apply; they land with your first collection.

A code is checked when you apply it and again when the order is made: it must be active, in date, for this purchase, and not used up or already used by your account. Some codes are for a first payment only. Internal Decibyl accounts cannot redeem codes.

### When plan credits run out

Voice minutes are then charged from your top-up pool at **one credit a minute more** — a Business account pays 14 instead of 13, Growth 13 instead of 12. Scale, the last rung, never pays more: 11 all month. Text, knowledge and routine work continues at its usual rate.

Credit appears once the payment is confirmed by Razorpay's servers, not when the payment window closes. That is usually a second or two. The balance on screen re-reads itself rather than assuming — a browser saying "paid" is not proof of payment.

<Note>
GST is added at checkout on top of the credit you buy. Buying ₹500 of credit charges ₹590 and gives you ₹500 of usage; the ₹90 is tax, not a fee.
</Note>

## What a credit buys

Every event a bot performs is either charged in credits or included in your plan. The thread shows the figure on each card.

| Event | Credits |
|---|---:|
| Voice, per minute (Everyday bundle) | 13 / 12 / 11 on Business / Growth / Scale |
| Text reply in a channel or chat | 1 |
| Knowledge answer (the reply read your documents) | 2; when the documents have nothing on it, the reply is 1 |
| Translation or transliteration | 1 per 100 characters, rounded up per message |
| Routine run | 2 |
| Trigger run (a webhook event the bot acts on) | 1 |
| Task run (a task on the board a bot did) | 1 |
| Tool call through a connector | 1; 3 for a system of record |
| WhatsApp message from the platform sender | 2 |
| Builder message past the plan's monthly allowance (30 / 100 / 300; unlimited on Scale) | 5 |
| Verifying a phone number by voice code, after the first two numbers | 2 |
| Knowledge pages past the plan's cap (50 / 500 / 2,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 held), typed | 1 per 10 pages |
| Knowledge pages past the cap, scanned (read by OCR) | 2 per page |

Knowledge pages are counted on what you hold: deleting a document gives its pages back. The upload screen says where you stand against the cap and what a page past it costs before the upload runs; the charge lands once the document is processed and its pages are known. Query-time retrieval is inside the 2-credit knowledge answer, and re-embedding when a model changes is free.

**Systems of record** are the connectors your business runs on, and a tool call into one is 3 credits: CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Bigin, Freshsales, LeadSquared), ERP and accounting (SAP, Tally, Zoho Books and Invoice, QuickBooks), commerce and logistics (Shopify, WooCommerce, Shiprocket, Delhivery), payments (Razorpay, Stripe, Cashfree) and helpdesks (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Zoho Desk). Google Workspace, email, Slack, Notion, Calendly, Airtable and the rest are 1.

Call events on the timeline — started, answered, ended, what the caller wanted, the credits held and settled — are included: the call itself is what is charged. Test chats and test calls inside the app are not charged.

## Invoices and receipts

Every payment produces a **receipt voucher** — a GST document with your registered name, GSTIN, place of supply, SAC 998314 and the tax split. Prices are shown exclusive of GST: 18% on plans, top-ups and overage, as CGST 9% + SGST 9% when your registered state is Tamil Nadu and IGST 18% otherwise.

**GSTIN at checkout.** Everyday can be bought without one. Business and above are invoiced as a business supply, so your GSTIN under **Billing → Billing details** is required before the plan can start; every plan needs your legal name, address and state first, because the first collection issues a document made out to them.

**Enterprise invoices.** Add your purchase-order number and agreed payment terms under Billing details and they print on every document. Our Udyam (MSME) registration number prints on every invoice.

**Refunds and corrections** are issued as a **credit note** against the original document, in its own numbered series, reversing the tax in the same proportion.

**Outside India.** Text-only plans are sold in US dollars as a zero-rated export of services under our Letter of Undertaking: the invoice states "Supply meant for export under LUT without payment of IGST", carries no GST line, and shows your country and address.

They appear under **Billing → Documents**, where each one can be downloaded as a PDF or sent to your billing email.

### Complete your billing profile first

Under **Billing → Billing details**, fill in your registered business name, GSTIN, address and billing email.

This matters more than it looks:

- **Your GSTIN and state code decide the tax split.** A customer in the same state as us pays CGST + SGST; anywhere else in India pays IGST. The total is the same either way, but the split is what your accountant files.
- **A document issued before you complete this has no customer details on it**, which makes it useless for claiming input credit.
- **Your billing email is where documents are sent.** Without one they are still issued and downloadable, but nothing is emailed.

If documents were issued before you filled this in, open **Billing → Documents** and use the send button on each one — they will go to your billing email with the PDF attached.

## Running out of credit

When your balance runs low you get an email warning before anything stops, sent once a day at 09:00 IST to everyone on the account.

**Calling pauses once your balance falls below ₹20**, and resumes the moment you top up. Nothing is deleted — your agents, numbers, recordings and transcripts stay exactly as they are.

The floor is ₹20 rather than zero on purpose. A call's cost is not known until it ends, so an account allowed to start a call on its last rupee would finish that call overdrawn. Stopping a little above zero means the last call you place is always one you had the credit for.

For a **managed phone number**, the monthly rental is collected from your balance. If it cannot be collected the number is suspended after seven days and becomes eligible for release after 45 — which is why the warning email exists, and why autopay is worth setting up.

## Autopay

**Billing → Autopay** sets up a UPI mandate so the monthly number rental collects automatically, without you needing to remember a top-up.

The mandate is authorised once by you and covers the rental only. It does not top up your usage credit — that stays a deliberate act.

## Seeing where the money went

**Agent Runs** lists every call with its duration and cost. **Analytics** aggregates that by day, agent and campaign.

If a number surprises you, start with the run: it shows the per-call breakdown of minutes, model usage and telephony, and the credits each part took.
