Decibyl does not show prices in the app. When a payment is made, the documents for it are issued as described below, and they stay under **Billing** for as long as the account exists.

## Invoices and receipts

Every payment produces a **receipt voucher** — a GST document with your registered name, GSTIN, place of supply, SAC 998314 and the tax split. Prices are shown exclusive of GST: 18% on plans, top-ups and overage, as CGST 9% + SGST 9% when your registered state is Tamil Nadu and IGST 18% otherwise.

**GSTIN at checkout.** Everyday can be bought without one. Business and above are invoiced as a business supply, so your GSTIN under **Billing → Billing details** is required before the plan can start; every plan needs your legal name, address and state first, because the first collection issues a document made out to them.

**Enterprise invoices.** Add your purchase-order number and agreed payment terms under Billing details and they print on every document. Our Udyam (MSME) registration number prints on every invoice.

**Refunds and corrections** are issued as a **credit note** against the original document, in its own numbered series, reversing the tax in the same proportion.

**Outside India.** Text-only plans are sold in US dollars as a zero-rated export of services under our Letter of Undertaking: the invoice states "Supply meant for export under LUT without payment of IGST", carries no GST line, and shows your country and address.

They appear under **Billing → Documents**, where each one can be downloaded as a PDF or sent to your billing email.

### Complete your billing profile first

Under **Billing → Billing details**, fill in your registered business name, GSTIN, address and billing email.

This matters more than it looks:

- **Your GSTIN and state code decide the tax split.** A customer in the same state as us pays CGST + SGST; anywhere else in India pays IGST. The total is the same either way, but the split is what your accountant files.
- **A document issued before you complete this has no customer details on it**, which makes it useless for claiming input credit.
- **Your billing email is where documents are sent.** Without one they are still issued and downloadable, but nothing is emailed.

If documents were issued before you filled this in, open **Billing → Documents** and use the send button on each one — they will go to your billing email with the PDF attached.
