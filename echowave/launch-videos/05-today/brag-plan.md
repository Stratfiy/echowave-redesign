# Brag Plan: Decibyl Today

## What is this app?
Today is Decibyl's second home screen: approvals waiting, what's due, the morning brief
and what's coming up, in one ordered list (`HomeToday.tsx`, screen 07).

## The angle
Most dashboards show you charts. Today shows you a to-do list your assistant has already
started. You drink your chai, approve three things, and the day's admin is done.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
6:58am, phone on the bedside table. A notification: "Good morning. 3 things need you."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Today list builds in order: approvals first, then due items, then the brief.
- Overnight work shows up as done: "Booked 2 appointments", "Answered 14 WhatsApp chats".
- Three approvals, each approved with a tap and each held long enough to read what's
  being approved.

## Outro / punchline
"Nothing due in Decibyl." (the real empty-state copy) on white, with the three pastel
blob faces around it. "Your day, started."

## User flow worth showing
Ask for a morning summary → Today lists approvals and what's done → tap to approve.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet morning ritual
- Interpretation: slow, warm, gentle crossfades

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 18s

## Visual identity (from the project)
Shared identity. The outro uses the white end frame with the three pastel blobs.

## Share copy (draft)
Most dashboards give you charts. Decibyl gives you a list it has already started: three
approvals, one tap each, before your chai gets cold.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, morning feel
- SFX posture: sparse. A notification, a soft tick per approval
- Restraint rule: nothing loud; this is the calmest video in the series

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — 6:58am — 3s
Lock screen, "6:58". Notification: "Good morning. 3 things need you."
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Already done — 4s
The Today list, "Done overnight": "Booked 2 appointments" · "Answered 14 WhatsApp chats" ·
"Reminded 9 customers about payments".
Sequential/interaction: rows arrive one by one, each held ≥0.8s
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Three taps — 7s
Three approvals, ~2.3s each, each tapped **Do it**:
- "Send Monday's price list to 6 dealers"
- "Pay Acme Print's invoice: ₹4,800"
- "Move Priya's appointment to 5:15pm"
Sequential/interaction: yes, simulated taps; each card fully readable before the tap
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 4s
"Nothing due in Decibyl." on white with the pastel blobs. "Your day, started." Wordmark.
Transition mood: end

Total: 3 + 4 + 7 + 4 = **18s**

**Music mood for this video:** gentle, warm
**Audio summary:** a soft alarm-free morning: one notification, three ticks, a warm fade.
