# Brag Plan: Decibyl Payment Reminder Agent

## What is this app?
The Payment Reminder Agent (`packs/live/payment_reminder`) calls before the due date,
takes the promise to pay, and never calls outside legal hours.

## The angle
Nobody likes the collections call, not the person making it and not the person getting
it. Decibyl makes it once, politely, by the rules, and hands anything difficult to a
person. The video is about *restraint*: what the agent won't do.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big type: "Nobody likes this call." A list of 214 overdue accounts scrolls behind it.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The agent confirms it's speaking to the borrower before it mentions any money (the
  pack's "Verify" step). If someone else answers, it says nothing about the loan.
- It states the amount and due date once, plainly, and asks one question: "Can you pay by
  the 15th?" Then it records the promise to pay.
- Three rules appear as plain cards: "Only between 8am and 7pm" · "Never tells anyone
  else" · "Disputes go to a person."

## Outro / punchline
A results strip: "182 promises to pay · 19 callbacks for your team · 0 calls after 7pm".
Then "Collections, done properly."

## User flow worth showing
Upload the list → agent calls within legal hours → promises and callbacks land in your list.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: serious and trustworthy, for a finance or lending buyer
- Interpretation: few scenes, steady holds, no humour about debt

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
Shared identity. Pink blob `#F7B5E3`. Results strip in ink on white; status colours from
the design handoff (`#075A39` success, `#705500` warning), each with a text label.

## Share copy (draft)
Decibyl makes the payment reminder call the way it should be made: only to the borrower,
only in legal hours, once and politely. And it hands disputes to your team.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents
- SFX posture: sparse. A soft tick per rule card, one tick per result count
- Restraint rule: no "cha-ching" or money sounds

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The problem — 3s
"Nobody likes this call." over a slow-scrolling list of 214 overdue accounts (fictional
names, amounts blurred).
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Verify first — 4.5s
Voice sheet, state "Speaking". Caption: "Am I speaking to Suresh?" → "Yes." Small tag:
"Identity confirmed before any detail."
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Once, plainly — 4.5s
Caption: "Your instalment of ₹6,200 is due on the 15th. Will you be able to pay by then?"
→ "Yes, on the 14th." A card: "Promise to pay · 14 Oct · ₹6,200".
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The rules — 4.5s
Three cards, one by one, each held ≥1s: "Only between 8am and 7pm" · "Never tells anyone
else" · "Disputes go to a person."
Sequential/interaction: yes, cards one by one, then held together
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Results — 3.5s
Count-up strip: "182 promises to pay · 19 callbacks · 0 calls after 7pm".
"Collections, done properly." Wordmark.
Transition mood: end

Total: 3 + 4.5 + 4.5 + 4.5 + 3.5 = **20s**

**Note:** the counts in scene 5 are illustrative and must be labelled "Example campaign"
on screen. Check the calling-hours wording against the pack's configured window before
rendering. **[founder sign-off]** for any compliance claim in public copy.

**Music mood for this video:** calm, steady
**Audio summary:** quiet confidence, ticks for each rule, a steady close.
