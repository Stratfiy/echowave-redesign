# Brag Plan: Decibyl Front Desk Agent

## What is this app?
The Front Desk Agent (`packs/live/front_desk_clinic`) answers every call, books the
appointment, and hands anything clinical to a person, in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu
or Kannada.

## The angle
The 8:47pm call. The clinic closed at 8, the receptionist has gone home, and a patient
is calling about tomorrow. Today that call is a lost patient. With Decibyl, the call
gets answered, the slot gets booked, and the owner sees it on Today in the morning.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A clock reads **8:47 PM** next to a "Closed" sign. A phone rings. Caption: "Missed calls
are missed patients." Then the blob face answers.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The caller speaks Tamil; the agent replies in Tamil, with English captions.
- The agent offers only open slots (the pack never offers a time you are closed) and
  confirms the booking.
- The caller asks something clinical ("Is this tooth pain serious?"), and the agent says
  a dentist will call back. This shows it knows where to stop.

## Outro / punchline
Morning, on Today: "1 appointment booked overnight · 1 callback for Dr. Rao". Then
"Your front desk, after hours."

## User flow worth showing
Call comes in → agent books in the caller's language → it shows on Today.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: a small-business owner's relief; warm, practical
- Interpretation: clean feature-card reveals, readable captions, nothing flashy

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
Shared identity (see `../README.md`). Use the yellow blob `#FFD66B` for this agent.

## Share copy (draft)
It's 8:47pm and your clinic is closed. Decibyl still picks up, books the appointment in
the patient's language, and puts it on your list for the morning.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- SFX posture: sparse. A ring, a booking confirmation chime, a soft morning tick
- Audio-coupled moments: the ring in the hook; the chime when the booking card appears
- Restraint rule: no voiceover; the call is shown through captions and the audio meter

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — After hours — 3s
Dark-ink clock "8:47 PM", a small "Closed · Mon–Sat 9:30am–8pm" chip. A phone ring.
Caption: "Missed calls are missed patients."
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Answered — 5s
Voice session sheet: yellow blob, "Speaking", one real audio meter.
Agent caption: "வணக்கம், Narayani Dental." / "Hello, Narayani Dental."
Caller caption: "நாளைக்கு ஒரு appointment வேணும்" / "I need an appointment tomorrow."
Sequential/interaction: captions alternate speaker by speaker, each held ≥1.2s
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Booked — 4.5s
Three slot chips appear: "10:00", "11:30", "5:15". "11:30" is picked, and a booking card
confirms: "Tomorrow, 11:30am · Cleaning · Priya S." Chime.
Sequential/interaction: yes, chips one by one, then a tap
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Knows when to hand off — 4s
Caller: "Is this pain serious?" Agent: "A dentist will call you back first thing
tomorrow. Is this the best number?" A small "Handed to a person" tag appears.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Morning — 3.5s
Today list: "1 appointment booked overnight" · "1 callback: Priya S., tooth pain".
End type: "Your front desk, after hours." Wordmark.
Transition mood: end

Total: 3 + 5 + 4.5 + 4 + 3.5 = **20s**

**Music mood for this video:** warm, quietly upbeat
**Audio summary:** a ring in the dark, a calm conversation, a chime, a bright morning.
