# Brag Plan: Decibyl answers staff from your documents

## What is this app?
The internal knowledge agent (`packs/live/internal_knowledge`) answers staff questions
from your own documents, shows the source, and says so when the answer isn't there.

## The angle
"Ask Meena, she knows." Every company has a Meena, and she's answering the same ten
questions all day. Decibyl reads the handbook so Meena doesn't have to, and it cites the
page.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Five chat pings stack up, all saying "@Meena quick question…". Caption: "Every team has
a Meena."

## Key moments (the middle)
- Drop three PDFs into the chat (Leave policy, Travel policy, Price list). They show as
  file chips with a "Reading" status.
- A question gets an answer with a source chip: "Leave policy, p. 4". The 360px source
  panel slides open with the line highlighted.
- A question that isn't in the documents gets an honest "Not in your documents. Want me
  to ask HR?", not a made-up answer.

## Outro / punchline
"Meena's back to her actual job." Wordmark.

## User flow worth showing
Attach documents → ask → answer with source → honest "not found".

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: a light office comedy with a useful payoff
- Interpretation: one gentle joke (Meena), then straight, clear demonstration

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18s

## Visual identity (from the project)
Shared identity. Yellow blob `#FFD66B`. Source panel per screen 04 (360px, opened on demand).

## Share copy (draft)
Every team has a Meena who answers the same ten questions all day. Decibyl reads your
documents, answers with the page it came from, and says when it doesn't know.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- SFX posture: sparse. Ping stack in the hook, a soft drop for files, a click opening the source
- Restraint rule: no sound on the "not found" moment; let it be calm

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Meena — 2.5s
Five pings stack: "@Meena quick question". Caption: "Every team has a Meena."
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Drop the documents — 3.5s
Three file chips land in the composer: "Leave policy.pdf", "Travel policy.pdf",
"Price list.xlsx". Status changes "Reading" → "Ready".
Sequential/interaction: yes, files drop one by one
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Answer with a source — 5s
"How many casual leaves do I get?" → "12 a year, up to 3 in a row." A source chip
"Leave policy, p. 4" is clicked, and the source panel opens with the line highlighted.
Sequential/interaction: simulated click on the source chip
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Honest when it doesn't know — 4s
"Can I carry leave into next year?" → "That isn't in your documents. Want me to ask HR?"
with a follow-up chip "Ask HR".
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3s
"Meena's back to her actual job." Wordmark.
Transition mood: end

Total: 2.5 + 3.5 + 5 + 4 + 3 = **18s**
