# Brag Plan: Decibyl on WhatsApp

## What is this app?
Decibyl answers customer enquiries on WhatsApp (`website_whatsapp_enquiry_desk`,
`listing_enquiry_responder`) with your prices, hours and stock, and no phone number is
needed (`message` direction).

## The angle
Forty unread chats on a Monday. Each one is a customer who will buy from whoever replies
first. Decibyl replies first, every time, and passes on the ones that need you.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A WhatsApp badge counts up fast: 3… 12… 41 unread. Caption: "Whoever replies first, wins."

## Key moments (the middle)
- Three chats get answered one after another: price, opening hours, "is the 2BHK still
  available?"
- One reply is in Hindi, one in English, one mixed (Hinglish), the way customers actually type.
- One chat needs a person, so Decibyl asks you: "Offer 5% off to Rahul?" with an approval card.

## Outro / punchline
The badge counts down to 0. "Every chat answered. The hard ones, asked."

## User flow worth showing
Message arrives → answer from your own facts → hard case comes to you as an approval.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: a satisfying inbox-zero moment
- Interpretation: brisk, playful, one idea per beat

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 18s

## Visual identity (from the project)
Shared identity. Green blob `#CDEB7A` for this agent. Keep the chat bubbles in Decibyl's
own style, with no WhatsApp logo or green; label the channel in text ("WhatsApp").

## Share copy (draft)
41 unread WhatsApp chats on a Monday. Decibyl answers them all, in the language your
customers type in, and asks you about the tricky ones.

## Audio direction
- Role: playful bed
- SFX posture: moderate. A pop per message, a soft tick as the badge counts down
- Restraint rule: pops only on arrivals; no sound on every streamed word

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The pile-up — 2.5s
Unread badge counts 3 → 41. Caption: "Whoever replies first, wins."
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Answered, one by one — 7s
Three short threads, each ~2.3s:
- "Price for the 6-seater dining set?" → "₹38,500, with free delivery in Pune."
- "आज दुकान कितने बजे तक खुली है?" → "आज रात 9 बजे तक।" (caption: "Open till 9pm tonight.")
- "2BHK Baner still available?" → "Yes. Want a visit on Saturday at 11 or 4?"
Sequential/interaction: yes, each reply streams in, then holds ≥1.2s
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — It asks you — 5s
Rahul: "Can you do a better price?" The ApprovalDock card: "Decibyl wants to: Offer
Rahul 5% off (₹1,925)". Tap **Do it**. Reply sent.
Sequential/interaction: simulated tap
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Inbox zero — 3.5s
Badge counts down to 0. "Every chat answered. The hard ones, asked." Wordmark.
Transition mood: end

Total: 2.5 + 7 + 5 + 3.5 = **18s**

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat
**Audio summary:** a rising pile of pops, a rhythm of answers, one tap, a clean finish.
