# Brag Plan: Decibyl (launch film)

## What is this app?
One assistant for your home, work and business. It takes calls and replies on WhatsApp
in your language, and it asks before it does anything that matters.

## The angle
Every AI assistant promises to "do things for you". Decibyl's difference is the
moment *before* it does them: an exact preview, the real recipient and amount, and one
tap to approve. The film shows a busy day of work being done and stops on the approval
card each time. **It does the work, and it asks first.**

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A phone rings on a white screen. A pastel blob face appears and picks it up. Caption:
"Narayani Dental, good morning." Within two seconds the viewer knows it answers calls.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Three jobs land as cards, one by one, all from the real Chat starters: "Answer my
  phone and book appointments", "Reply to customers on WhatsApp", "Chase overdue payments".
- A Hindi WhatsApp reply streams into a thread with an English caption underneath, to
  show "in your language".
- The approval card: "Decibyl wants to: Pay Acme Print's invoice: ₹4,800" with a tap
  on **Do it**, then "Sent" with evidence. This is the centrepiece.

## Outro / punchline
"It does the work. It asks first." Then the Decibyl wordmark over the calm Chat home,
greeting "Good morning", and a blinking composer.

## User flow worth showing
Type a request in Chat → Decibyl works ("Decibyl is working on it.") → "Decibyl is
waiting for your approval." → Do it → done.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a calm, confident product film; quiet premium, not a hype reel
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, soft crossfades. The calm is the message:
  an assistant you can trust with your phone line.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080 (plus a vertical 1080x1920 re-cut)
## Duration: 22s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#ffffff`
- Accent: blob pastels `#F7B5E3` / `#FFD66B` / `#CDEB7A`; `#df8e1d` only on "waiting for your approval"
- Text: `#0d0d0d`, body `#5d5d5d`
- Display font: Lato (heavy)
- Body font: Lato
- Strongest visual element: the BlobFace agent avatars and the ApprovalDock "waiting" card

## Share copy (draft)
Decibyl answers your phone, replies on WhatsApp and chases payments, in your language.
And it asks before it does anything that matters.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse, motion-matched accents
- Music: soft, upbeat, unhurried; fade in under the ring
- Music treatment: low volume until the approval tap, a small lift at the outro
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- SFX posture: sparse. A phone ring (scene 1), soft card arrivals (scene 2), one clean tap (scene 4)
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no bass drops, no voiceover; captions carry the words

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The call — 3.5s
White canvas. A phone ring. A pink blob face fades in at centre and its eyes blink.
Live-voice sheet: state label "Listening", one real audio meter, caption
"Narayani Dental, good morning. How can I help?"
Sequential/interaction: caption appears word-group by word-group as it is spoken
Audio intent: a familiar ring, then calm
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Three jobs — 5s
The Chat home. Three starter chips arrive one at a time, each with its own blob colour:
"Answer my phone and book appointments" / "Reply to customers on WhatsApp" /
"Chase overdue payments". Hold all three together for 1.5s.
Sequential/interaction: yes, three chips one by one, ~0.8s apart, then held
Audio intent: three soft card ticks
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — In your language — 4s
A WhatsApp-style thread. Customer: "कल शाम को अपॉइंटमेंट मिलेगा?" Decibyl's reply
streams in Hindi, with the English caption below it: "Yes, 6:30pm tomorrow is free.
Shall I book it?"
Sequential/interaction: reply text streams (real streaming, not fake typing)
Audio intent: quiet message pop
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — It asks first — 5.5s
The ApprovalDock "waiting" card under the composer, amber "Waiting for your approval".
Title: "Decibyl wants to: Pay Acme Print's invoice: ₹4,800". Buttons as shipped: **Do it**, **Don't**,
"See everything it will do". Cursor taps **Do it** → "Approved: Pay Acme Print's invoice.
It runs in a few seconds." → "Sent", with a small evidence line.
Sequential/interaction: yes, simulated tap and state change
Audio intent: one clean, satisfying tap; the music lifts slightly
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 4s
Large type: "It does the work." (1.2s), then "It asks first." (1.5s). Crossfade to the
Chat home: "Good morning", composer, Decibyl wordmark, `app.decibyl.ai`.
Audio intent: the music resolves and fades
Transition mood: end

Total: 3.5 + 5 + 4 + 5.5 + 4 = **22s**

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat but calm
**Audio summary:** a ring, a warm bed, three ticks, one decisive tap, a gentle resolve.

**Vertical re-cut:** same scenes; stack the chips in scene 2 vertically and make the
approval card fill the width.
