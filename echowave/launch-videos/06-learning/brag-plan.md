# Brag Plan: Learn with Decibyl

## What is this app?
"Teach me something" starts a learning session inside Chat (screen 13): a short
explanation, one practice prompt, feedback, in your language and at your pace.

## The angle
Decibyl isn't only for business. The same assistant that runs your front desk teaches
your mother to spot a scam call, in Telugu, one question at a time. This shows the
personal side of "one assistant for your whole life".

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A phone screen: "Your bank account is blocked. Share OTP to unblock." Caption: "Would
your mother know this is a scam?"

## Key moments (the middle)
- She types "Teach me how to spot scam calls" in Telugu. The lesson opens with a goal
  header and a short explanation.
- A practice prompt: three messages, one of them real. She taps the scam one. Feedback:
  "Right. Banks never ask for an OTP."
- Progress: "Lesson 2 of 5 · Next: fake delivery messages." The next lesson is picked
  from what she got wrong.

## Outro / punchline
"Learn anything. In your language. At your pace." Wordmark.

## User flow worth showing
Ask to learn → short lesson → practice → feedback → next step chosen for you.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: warm, family, reassuring
- Interpretation: unhurried, large readable type (simple-mode feel), generous holds

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
Shared identity, with larger type (closer to `simple-mode.css`). Pink blob `#F7B5E3`.
Telugu text uses a Noto fallback at line height 1.6 (design handoff, Indic text rule).

## Share copy (draft)
Decibyl taught my mother to spot a scam call, in Telugu, one question at a time. Same
assistant that answers the shop's phone.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- SFX posture: sparse. A soft "correct" chime on the right answer
- Restraint rule: no alarm or danger sounds on the scam message

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The scam — 3s
Fake SMS: "Your bank account is blocked. Share OTP to unblock." Caption: "Would your
mother know this is a scam?"
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Ask — 4s
Chat composer, large type. "స్కామ్ కాల్స్ ఎలా గుర్తించాలో నేర్పించు" with the caption
"Teach me how to spot scam calls". The lesson header appears: "Goal: spot a scam in 10 seconds".
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Practice — 6s
Three message cards, one by one: a real bank alert, a delivery update, the OTP scam.
She taps the scam. Feedback block: "Right. Banks never ask for an OTP." Chime.
Sequential/interaction: yes, cards one by one, then a simulated tap
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Her pace — 3.5s
Progress line: "Lesson 2 of 5 · Next: fake delivery messages".
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3.5s
"Learn anything. In your language. At your pace." Wordmark.
Transition mood: end

Total: 3 + 4 + 6 + 3.5 + 3.5 = **20s**

**Note:** have a native Telugu speaker check every line before rendering (design
handoff: "native-language QA").

**Music mood for this video:** warm, gentle
**Audio summary:** a quiet start, one happy chime, a warm close.
