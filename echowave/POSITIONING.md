# Positioning

The one place the product's description, tagline and public copy are written
down. Anything that describes Decibyl — a README, a page title, the sign-in
screen, the docs, a deck — takes its words from here. `AGENTS.md` still holds:
no new plan, price or positioning string without the founder.

## The vision

**Personal intelligence that grows and evolves with you.**

One assistant for a person's whole life — home, work and business — that acts
on the phone and WhatsApp in their language, teaches them at their own pace,
remembers what matters, and always asks before it acts.

## What each word is standing on

| Claim | What makes it true | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Personal | Personal space, memory per person, private cards only its owner sees | `IDENTITY.md`, `CONTROLS.md` |
| Grows with you | Confirmed-only memory that compacts and keeps late corrections; learning journeys paced to the person | `LEARNING.md`, memory fixes (#571, #574) |
| Evolves with you | Decibyl builds and edits agents from one line; new helpers join as needs change | #577, `AGENTS-LAUNCH.md` |
| Home | Today brief, reminders, care for older people, ordering, private browser | `CARE.md`, `REACH.md` |
| Work | Inbox, research, follow-up, meetings, its own email address and number | `MEETINGS.md`, `IDENTITY.md` |
| Business | Agents hired for a job, shared with the team; phone and WhatsApp | `AGENTS-LAUNCH.md`, `packs/` |
| In their language | Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and eight more, including mid-sentence switching | `docs/getting-started/index.mdx`, `VOICE.md` |
| Asks before it acts | Every send, call, booking or payment is an exact preview a person confirms | `LAUNCH-PLAN.md` rule 2 |

If a feature behind a claim is still behind a switch at launch, the copy that
leans on it waits with it.

## decibyl.ai — hero (draft for the founder)

`landing/` is empty until a marketing site exists. When it does, this is the
draft above the fold.

> **Personal intelligence that grows and evolves with you.**
>
> One assistant for home, work and business. Decibyl answers the phone,
> replies on WhatsApp and email, and gets things done in the tools you already
> use — in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and eight more. It learns what matters to you,
> and it always asks before it acts.
>
> [ Request an invite ]   [ See what it does ]

Three columns under the hero:

- **At home.** A brief every morning, reminders that keep their time, help
  learning anything at your own pace, and a careful eye on parents — medicines,
  scam calls, simple mode.
- **At work.** It clears the inbox, researches, follows up, and takes notes in
  meetings. It has its own email address and number, so it can act for you
  without using yours.
- **In your business.** Hire an agent for a job — the front desk, order
  confirmations, payment reminders — and share it with your team. It answers
  on the phone and WhatsApp, day and night.

Trust line under the columns:

> Nothing is sent, booked, called or paid until you say yes. Your memory is
> yours: see it, correct it, or erase it.

## What changed

Until October 2026 Decibyl was described as a voice-AI platform for Indian
SMBs, and later as an agent platform for Indian businesses. Both are now the
**business** third of the product. Voice is a channel, not the product.
`PRD.md` sections 2–5 still describe that business voice channel in depth.
