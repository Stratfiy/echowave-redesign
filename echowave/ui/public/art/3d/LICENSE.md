# 3D icons

Source: 3dicons, https://3dicons.co, by Vijay Verma (https://github.com/realvjy).

License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication),
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. No attribution is
required; this note records where the files came from.

Fetched 24 Sep 2026 from the 3dicons CDN (dynamic angle, color style,
https://3dicons.sgp1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/v1/dynamic/color/<name>-dynamic-color.png)
and saved here as 400px WebP with a transparent background. Four files are
stored under a clearer name than the CDN's: shield (sheild), tools (tool),
call-ringing (phone-ringing), credit-card (card). crown had no transparent
original on the CDN; its white background was keyed out here.
tea-cup and notebook were shifted in hue here, from indigo to warm orange
and to clear blue, to keep violet out of the product's palette.

Not used in the product (purple-tinted): lab, chart, bulb, magic-trick, chat-text.
