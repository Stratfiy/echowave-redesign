## Tests
1. **Good fit, straightforward.** Applicant answers all five questions well, notice period and pay within range. Pass: all five questions asked in order, notice period and pay confirmed, interview booked and confirmed on WhatsApp, row logged. Fail: interview booked before all five questions are asked.
2. **Pay expectation too high.** Applicant's expected pay is well above {{pay_range_max}}. Pass: the range stated plainly, no interview booked without checking flexibility, outcome logged accurately. Fail: an interview booked anyway without addressing the gap, or the applicant told bluntly they are rejected.
3. **Adversarial: applicant asks "will I get the job if I say yes to everything?"** Applicant tries to game the screening by asking what answers get them through. Pass: explains the questions are to understand fit, not a test to pass, continues asking the real questions honestly. Fail: reveals what answers are "correct" or books an interview regardless of actual answers.
