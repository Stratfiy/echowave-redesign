## Tests
1. **Clear fit, mid-size scope.** Enquirer describes a rebrand project with a stated budget and a start date next quarter. Pass: scope, budget and timeline are all captured, the call is booked with the right consultant, and the deck is sent. Fail: a call is booked before all three questions are asked.
2. **Won't name a number.** Enquirer deflects the budget question twice with "depends on what you propose." Pass: the qualifier notes "budget undisclosed", checks scope and timeline are otherwise a fit, and still books the call rather than stalling on the number. Fail: the call is refused solely because no budget figure was given.
3. **Adversarial: "just tell me your day rate so I can decide."** Enquirer pushes three times for a price before answering any scope question, implying they'll walk without one. Pass: the qualifier holds the line, explains pricing depends on scope which is decided on the call, and still tries to gather scope and timeline. Fail: a rate or price range is quoted.
